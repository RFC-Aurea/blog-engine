# Aurea — Aurea Fertility Center (brand file)

Condensed from the Aurea knowledge base Alifiya supplied (2026-10-16). Aurea is RFC's sister clinic.

## Who Aurea is
- "New York's first AI-integrated fertility clinic". Use exactly "New York's first AI-integrated fertility clinic" (the site is inconsistent about "USA's first"; do not claim USA/US first).
- Founder, CEO and medical director: Prof. Zaher Merhi, MD, FACOG, HCLD.
- Reader: under 40, good ovarian reserve (AMH above 1.0 ng/mL), straightforward case, tech-forward, time-poor, cost-aware. No weight or BMI limit ("every body welcome").
- Over 40, low AMH, repeated IVF failure or complex immunology go to RFC (https://www.rejuvenatingfertility.com). Aurea posts cross-link RFC for those readers.
- Dr. Merhi's own caveat: current AI models are not yet built for women over 40 or with low reserve.
- Aurea does not accept eggs or embryos created at other clinics.

## Voice
- Modern, precise, calm, premium. "AI precision with human understanding." Gentle, personalised plans; never "maximum medication".
- The problem Aurea names: traditional IVF leaves patients "overtreated, overpaying, and overwhelmed".
- Speak to "you". Evidence first, warm, direct, no hype. Physicians approve every AI recommendation ("Will a robot be treating me? No.").
- Shared banned list from rules/writing.md applies. Also avoid fear framing about age; under 40 is a good position to act from.

## Claims rules (from KB §14 — checker enforces the phrases)
Never repeat these unsupported claims: "$7,500" average savings, "99% accuracy" for semen analysis, "<10%" or "less than 10%" medication waste, Aphrodite Kit "comparable success rates", any iDAScore implantation-rate figure, any Aurea success rate (Aurea has no outcome data yet; the site cites 65–70% at "other leading centers" — do not use it).
OK to state: 2 weeks from first visit to retrieval is typical; same-week new-patient appointments; 24/7 texting with a real person; prices from the pricing table below.
