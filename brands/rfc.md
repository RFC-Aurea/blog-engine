## Who RFC is
- Manhattan and Connecticut's first non-conventional IVF center. Founded by Dr. Zaher Merhi.
- The sentence everything comes from: **"When others say no, we specialize in hard cases. No age cutoff. No weight cutoff."**
- RFC's reader has often already been turned away by another clinic. She is usually over 40, has low AMH / diminished ovarian reserve, POI, prior IVF failures, recurrent loss, PCOS or a high BMI.
- Under-40 / AMH > 1 / straightforward cases belong to the sister clinic Aurea (https://www.aureafertility.com). RFC posts may cross-link Aurea for young, straightforward readers.

## Voice: hope first (rule #1)
1. Every post leaves the reader with a next step at RFC.
2. Lead with what is possible, then context. The Quick Answer opens with possibility.
3. Every difficulty is paired in the same breath with the RFC service that addresses it.
4. Never talk a patient out of care or editorialize on whether treatment is "worth it".
5. Age is a challenge RFC solves, never a verdict. Over 40 is RFC's specialty.
6. Statistics serve her next step. Context every number against what RFC offers.
7. Donor eggs, surrogacy and rejuvenation are wins, not consolation prizes.
8. "Hard" is allowed. "Hopeless" never is. Register: "even though it is hard, there is always a good chance".
- Tone: warm, confident, authoritative. Treat her as capable and well-read. Confidence, not cheerleading.

## Banned in RFC posts (checker enforces)
futile, futility, too late, the door is closed, give up, not worth pursuing, your only option, unfortunately, sadly, the hard truth, hopeless, no chance, refusing to sell you cycles. Any framing where a national average or another clinic is the authority on her prognosis.
